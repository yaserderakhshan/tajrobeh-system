Persian (Farsi) speaking therapist online, wherever you live

Tajrobeh (Life Experience Center) is an Iranian mental health clinic founded in Tehran in 2019. We offer online psychotherapy in Persian for Iranians and Farsi speakers abroad, with therapists based in Iran, Germany, the UK, the US and Australia, so sessions are scheduled in your own time zone.

  • Sessions cost €25 to €35 (€25 for students), paid per session.
  • The first introductory session with your therapist is free.
  • Payment by Stripe or PayPal with your local card; no Iranian bank account needed.
  • In-person sessions available at our partner clinic in Germany.
  • Every therapist is interviewed, has had personal therapy, and works under clinical supervision.
