Living in Germany

What makes life in Germany hard, and why language matters

These are the things Iranians in Germany tell us in the introductory session. None of them is an illness; they are pressures that need to be heard in your own language.

German works for the office, not for the heart

Your German may be B2 or C1 and enough for work and the Ausländerbehörde. Talking about fear, shame or a relationship in a language you have no feeling for still cuts half of what you mean.

Termine, letters and waiting

Residence permits, recognition of your degree, the Bürgeramt, finding a flat. Each comes with weeks or months of uncertainty, and the uncertainty wears you down more than the work itself.

Long grey winters, far from family

Short days from November to March and a family a few time zones away. Bad news arrives from a distance, and there is little you can do about it.

Frequently asked

What Iranians in Germany ask before they start

How do I find an Iranian psychologist in Germany?

Leave your number in the form on this page. Tajrobeh reception messages you the same day, asks what is going on and which hours suit you, and introduces a few Persian-speaking therapists: our therapist based in Germany, or therapists in Iran whose hours match yours. The introductory session with the therapist is free.

How much does a Persian-speaking therapist cost in Germany?

At Tajrobeh a session costs €25 for students and €35 for everyone else, fixed in euros. You pay with a German bank card via Stripe or PayPal; no Iranian bank account is needed. A private session with a therapist based in Germany, outside insurance, usually starts at around €70.

Does German health insurance cover it?

Public insurance (GKV) does not, because our therapists are not registered in the German insurance system. Some private or supplementary insurers reimburse part of the cost. You receive an official receipt for every session.

Is the session in Persian or German?

Sessions are in Persian. If you prefer to say some things in German or English, write it in the form and we will introduce a therapist who is also fluent in that language.

What is the time difference?

With our therapist based in Germany, none. Therapists in Iran are one and a half to two and a half hours ahead of Germany, so their working hours cover roughly your morning to early evening.

Can I see someone in person in Germany?

Yes. Tajrobeh has an in-person partner clinic in Germany, and you can combine in-person and online sessions. Message reception for the address and available times.

You do not need to know what the problem is yet

That is exactly what the introductory session is for: a short conversation with a therapist who understands your language, free and with no obligation. If it fits, you continue; if not, you have lost nothing.
